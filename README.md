> The hallway light went out on the third step.
> I stood still, one hand on the rail.
> The dark said nothing. It did not move.
> My palm found the rail again, cool and steady.
> Below, the kitchen door stood open.
> The step below was where it had always been.
> I took it.

A short text for make.fun: the light goes out on the stairs, the dark holds still, and one small step is all the courage it takes. Fear, a patient wait, the dark holding steady — then one small brave step at the end. The entry program (`main.ts`, Node 22, stdlib only) writes the text exactly as written to `entry.txt`.
